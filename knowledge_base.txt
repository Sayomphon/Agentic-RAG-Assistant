--- International Travel Approval Process ---
Employees traveling internationally for business must obtain written approval
from their department head at least 14 days before departure. All requests are
submitted through the TravelHub system under "Overseas Trip Request" and must
include a trip purpose statement, an itinerary, and a cost estimate. Trips
longer than 10 business days additionally require sign-off from the Managing
Director. Once approved, the Travel Desk books economy-class airfare through
the company's contracted agency, ThaiOrbit Travel. Self-booked flights are not
reimbursable unless the Travel Desk confirms in writing that no contracted
option was available. Visa fees and airport transfers are arranged by the
Travel Desk and charged directly to the department's cost center.

--- International Travel Daily Allowance ---
For approved international business trips, Siam Innovate provides a daily
allowance of 2,400 THB to cover meals and incidental expenses. The allowance
is paid per full calendar day abroad; departure and return days are paid at
half rate (1,200 THB). Hotel accommodation is booked by the Travel Desk with
a cap of 5,500 THB per night in Southeast Asia and 8,000 THB per night
elsewhere. No receipts are required for the daily allowance itself, but hotel
and ground transport receipts must be uploaded to ExpenseFlow within 45 days
of the return date. Allowances are deposited together with the following
month's payroll.

--- International Travel Insurance ---
All international business trips are automatically covered by the company's
group travel insurance, the SafeJourney Plan B underwritten by Bangkok
Assurance PCL. Coverage includes emergency medical treatment up to
3,000,000 THB, emergency evacuation, and lost-luggage compensation up to
40,000 THB per trip. Employees must carry the digital insurance card, which
is available in the HR mobile app (SiamPeople) under "My Benefits". Claims
must be filed through the SafeJourney portal within 30 days of returning,
together with original receipts and a copy of the trip approval from
TravelHub. Personal side-trips extending a business trip are not covered
beyond 48 hours after the last business day.

--- Remote Work Policy ---
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Remote work requests are submitted through the FlexWork portal by Thursday
of the preceding week. Approval is at the manager's discretion and may be
withdrawn if collaboration or performance suffers. Remote employees must be
reachable on Microsoft Teams during their agreed working hours and must use
the TigerLink VPN when accessing internal systems. New hires in their first
30 calendar days are expected to work fully on-site to support onboarding.
Working remotely from outside Thailand for more than 5 consecutive business
days requires prior written approval from both HR and the Legal team.

--- Hybrid Work Guidelines ---
Siam Innovate operates a hybrid model: staff can work off-site up to 3 days
each week, subject to manager approval requested via the FlexWork portal no
later than Thursday of the week before. In addition, all employees observe
core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 16:00 (Bangkok time), during which
they must be available for meetings regardless of location. Tuesdays are
designated company-wide anchor days, when all teams work from the Rama IX
headquarters. Teams may define one additional anchor day of their own. Desk
booking for office days is done through the SpaceLy app at least one day in
advance.

--- Annual Leave ---
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued at 1.25 days per month. After five full years of service, the
entitlement increases to 20 days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over
into the first quarter of the following year; carried-over days expire on
31 March. Leave requests are submitted in the LeaveDesk system at least
3 business days in advance for absences of 3 days or fewer, and at least
14 days in advance for longer absences. Managers must approve or decline
requests within 2 business days. Unused leave beyond the carry-over limit
is forfeited without compensation.

--- Sick Leave ---
Employees are entitled to paid sick leave of up to 30 working days per year,
in line with Thai labor law. Absences of 3 or more consecutive working days
require a medical certificate from a licensed physician, uploaded to
LeaveDesk within 5 days of returning to work. For same-day sick notice,
employees must inform their manager via Teams or phone before 10:00. Sick
leave cannot be converted to cash or carried over. Employees who exhaust
their sick leave entitlement may apply for unpaid medical leave, which
requires HR approval and, for absences beyond 30 additional days, an
assessment by the company's occupational health partner, MediTrust Clinic.

--- Parental Leave ---
Pregnant employees are entitled to 98 days of maternity leave per pregnancy,
of which 45 days are paid at full salary by the company and the remainder
covered under Social Security rules. Fathers employed by Siam Innovate
receive 15 working days of paid paternity leave, to be used within 90 days
of the child's birth. Employees adopting a child under age 7 receive 30
calendar days of paid adoption leave. All parental leave is coordinated
through HR at least 30 days before the expected start date where possible,
and recorded in LeaveDesk under the "Family" category. Returning parents may
request a temporary 4-day work week for up to 6 months, subject to manager
approval.

--- Expense Reimbursement ---
Business expenses are reimbursed through the ExpenseFlow system. Employees
must submit claims with scanned receipts within 45 days of the expense date;
late claims are rejected automatically unless the CFO grants an exception.
Claims under 20,000 THB are approved by the line manager; claims of
20,000 THB or more also require Finance approval. Approved amounts are paid
with the next payroll run. Non-reimbursable items include alcohol (except
approved client entertainment), traffic fines, minibar charges, and personal
subscriptions. Client entertainment above 5,000 THB per event requires
pre-approval from the department head. Mileage for personal-car business
trips is reimbursed at 6 THB per kilometer.

--- Corporate Credit Card ---
Employees at manager level and above may apply for a KrungSiam Platinum
corporate credit card through the Finance Service Desk. The standard monthly
limit is 150,000 THB; temporary increases for approved events or business
trips can be requested via ExpenseFlow at least 5 business days in advance.
Cardholders must reconcile all transactions in ExpenseFlow within 45 days,
attaching itemized receipts — the same deadline that applies to out-of-pocket
claims. Personal use of the corporate card is strictly prohibited and leads
to card cancellation on the second violation. Lost or stolen cards must be
reported to KrungSiam Bank's 24-hour hotline and the Finance Service Desk
within 24 hours.

--- IT Security and Password Policy ---
All company accounts must use passwords of at least 16 characters generated
and stored in the company password manager, ShieldPass. Multi-factor
authentication via the OktaSiam app is mandatory for email, ExpenseFlow,
and all production systems. Sharing credentials is a disciplinary offense.
Phishing simulations are run monthly by the Security team; employees who
fail three simulations in a rolling 12-month period must retake the
SecureBase awareness course. Suspected security incidents must be reported
to soc@siaminnovate.example within 1 hour of discovery. USB storage devices
are blocked by default on all company laptops and require a time-limited
exemption approved by the CISO.

--- VPN Usage ---
Access to internal systems from outside the office network requires the
TigerLink VPN client, installed by IT on all company devices. Employees sign
in with their ShieldPass-managed credentials plus OktaSiam multi-factor
authentication. The VPN session limit is 12 hours, after which
re-authentication is required. Split tunneling is disabled; all traffic
routes through the corporate gateway while connected. Personal devices may
not connect to TigerLink except under the BYOD program, which requires
device enrollment in the MobiGuard management platform and annual security
attestation. VPN connection issues are reported to the IT Service Desk via
the HelpHub portal, with a target response time of 4 business hours.

--- Onboarding Process ---
New employees join through the LaunchPad onboarding program. Before day one,
IT ships a configured laptop and HR sends a welcome pack through the
SiamPeople app. The first week includes orientation sessions on company
values, security (the SecureBase course must be completed within 7 days),
and tooling. Every new hire is paired with a LaunchPad buddy — a colleague
outside their direct team — for the first 60 days. Managers must schedule a
30-60-90 day check-in series in GrowthCompass. New hires work fully on-site
for their first 30 calendar days, after which standard hybrid rules apply.
Building access cards are issued by Facilities on day one at the Rama IX
reception.

--- Probation Period ---
All new employees serve a probation period of 119 days from their start
date. During probation, either party may terminate employment with 7 days'
written notice. A mid-probation review is held around day 60, and the final
confirmation review must be completed in GrowthCompass no later than day
105, so HR can process the outcome before the legal deadline. Probationary
employees accrue annual leave normally but may only take it after day 60,
except with special manager approval. Successful confirmation unlocks
benefits enrollment in the VitalCare Gold health plan and the provident
fund, both effective from the first day of the month following confirmation.

--- Performance Review Cycle ---
Performance is reviewed twice a year in GrowthCompass: a mid-year review in
June and a full-year review in December. Each cycle includes a self-review,
peer feedback from at least 2 colleagues, and a manager assessment against
the goals agreed at the start of the year. Ratings use a 4-point scale:
Exceptional, Strong, Developing, and Off-Track. Employees rated Off-Track
enter a 60-day performance improvement plan with biweekly check-ins. Salary
adjustments and bonus decisions follow the December cycle and take effect
with the February payroll. Promotion cases are prepared by managers and
reviewed by the cross-functional Talent Council, which meets each January
and July.

--- Training Budget ---
Each employee has an individual learning budget of 25,000 THB per calendar
year, managed through the SkillForge platform. The budget covers courses,
certifications, conferences, and professional books. Single items above
15,000 THB require manager approval in SkillForge before purchase.
Certification exam fees are covered in full and do not draw down the
personal budget if the certification appears on the company's Priority
Skills List, published each January by the L&D team. Unused budget does not
roll over. Employees who resign within 6 months of completing a
company-funded certification costing more than 20,000 THB must repay 50% of
the cost, as set out in the training agreement signed at enrollment.

--- Equipment and Laptop Policy ---
New employees choose between a MacBook Air and a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 as their
standard machine; engineering roles may request a higher-spec build via the
HelpHub portal. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, and the old device must
be returned to IT for certified data wiping. Employees receive a one-time
home-office allowance of 12,000 THB, claimable through ExpenseFlow within
the first 90 days of employment, covering a monitor, chair, or desk
accessories. All devices are enrolled in the MobiGuard management platform
and must not be altered or jailbroken. Damaged or lost equipment must be
reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours; negligent loss may result
in a cost-sharing charge of up to 20% of replacement value.

--- Overtime Policy ---
Non-exempt employees who work beyond standard hours with prior manager
approval are compensated per Thai labor law: 1.5x the hourly rate on
workdays, 2x for work on weekly holidays, and 3x for overtime on public
holidays. Overtime must be requested and approved in the TimeTrack system
before the work is performed; retroactive claims are accepted only for
documented production emergencies. Exempt employees (manager level and
above) are not eligible for overtime pay but may claim compensatory time
off for weekend releases, capped at 6 days per year. Sustained overtime
above 24 hours per month triggers a workload review between the manager
and HR Business Partner.

--- Resignation Process ---
Employees who wish to resign must give at least 30 days' written notice,
submitted through SiamPeople with a signed resignation letter. The final
working date is confirmed by HR after checking outstanding leave and
project handover plans. Departing employees complete a handover document
approved by their manager, return all equipment and access cards to IT and
Facilities, and attend an exit interview with the HR Business Partner in
the final week. Unused annual leave within the statutory entitlement is
paid out with the final salary. Access to all systems, including SiamPeople
and ExpenseFlow, is revoked at 18:00 on the last working day; pending
expense claims must be submitted before then.

--- Company Holidays ---
Siam Innovate observes 16 paid public holidays per year, published each
November for the following year on the SiamPeople app. The list always
includes New Year's Day, Songkran (13-15 April), Labour Day, and His
Majesty the King's Birthday. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, a
substitute day off is granted on the next working day. The office closes
early at 15:00 on the last working day before New Year and before Songkran.
Employees required to work on a public holiday due to production duty
receive holiday overtime rates per the Overtime Policy plus a substitute
day off to be used within 60 days.

--- Health Benefits ---
After probation confirmation, employees are enrolled in the VitalCare Gold
group health plan administered by Bangkok Assurance PCL. The plan covers
outpatient care up to 2,500 THB per visit (maximum 30 visits per year),
inpatient hospitalization up to 500,000 THB per condition, and annual dental
care up to 8,000 THB. An annual health checkup at BCH Wellness Center is
provided every January at no cost. Employees may add a spouse and up to
3 children to the plan at a subsidized premium of 4,200 THB per dependent
per year, deducted from payroll. Claims are submitted through the VitalCare
mobile app; cashless service is available at network hospitals listed in
the SiamPeople benefits page.
